## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

### Primary Target — Ecom / DTC Brand

  Revenue:          $2M — $50M ARR (7 to low 8 figure)
  Business model:   Direct to consumer, ecommerce, product brand
  Channels:         Shopify or similar, running paid ads (Meta/Google)
  Email situation:  Either not doing email properly, using a junior
                    in-house person, or on a cheap tool with no strategy
  Pain:             Leaving revenue on the table from their existing
                    customer base — over-relying on paid ads for growth
  What they want:   More revenue from email without more ad spend
  Decision maker:   Founder / CEO (under 50 staff)
                    OR Head of Marketing / CMO (larger brands)

### Secondary Target — Consumer Tech / SaaS with DTC motion

  Revenue:          $5M — $100M
  Business model:   Subscription or product-led with email as a
                    retention and upsell channel
  Pain:             Poor onboarding flows, high churn, weak LTV
  Decision maker:   Head of Growth, VP Marketing, Founder

## Bad Fit Signals (Exclude These)

  - Pure B2B SaaS with no consumer motion
  - Service businesses (agencies, consultants, law firms)
  - Marketplaces (they don't own the customer relationship)
  - Brands under $500K revenue (wrong budget)
  - Brands that already have a strong in-house email team
  - Other email marketing agencies (competitors)
  - Anyone who has posted negatively about email agencies on LinkedIn

## Outbound Sequence — Ecom Founder

  Build in: Apollo → Sequences → New Sequence
  Name: XYZ Ecom Founder Outreach — June 2026
  Enroll from: Search 1 list above

  ---

  Step 1 — Email (Day 1)
  Subject: {{first_name}} — quick one re email revenue

  Hey {{first_name}},

  Came across {{company_name}} — love what you're building
  in [industry/niche].

  Quick question: is email currently one of your top 3 revenue
  channels, or is it more of an afterthought right now?

  We work with DTC brands doing 7-8 figures and typically add
  15-30% more revenue from their existing list within 90 days —
  without touching their ad budget.

  Worth a 20 minute call to see if there's a fit?

  [Your name]
  XYZ

  ---

  Step 2 — LinkedIn connect (Day 3)
  Note: "Hey {{first_name}} — sent you a quick email, would be
  good to connect. Building something interesting at
  {{company_name}}."

  ---

  Step 3 — Email follow-up (Day 7)
  Subject: re: email revenue (reply to thread)

  Hey {{first_name}},

  Just bumping this up — worked with a DTC brand similar to
  {{company_name}} recently, helped them go from email driving
  8% of revenue to 34% in one quarter.

  Happy to share exactly what we did if useful.

  [Your name]

  ---

  Step 4 — LinkedIn message (Day 14)
  Only if connected.

  "Hey {{first_name}} — curious, what's your biggest challenge
  with retention right now? Trying to understand what brands
  in [their space] are dealing with."

  ---

  Step 5 — Final email (Day 21)
  Subject: closing the loop

  Hey {{first_name}},

  Not going to keep chasing — I know the timing might just
  not be right.

  If email revenue ever becomes a priority for {{company_name}},
  happy to pick this up. We're selective about who we work with
  so there'd be a proper fit conversation first anyway.

  Good luck with everything.

  [Your name]

## Sending Rules

  - Max 50 cold emails per day per sending domain
  - Only enroll contacts with Apollo email confidence: high
  - Warm the sending domain for 2 weeks before scaling up
    if it's a new domain
  - Set up SPF, DKIM, DMARC on XYZ's sending domain before
    launching any sequence
  - Never send from the main XYZ domain — use a subdomain
    or variation e.g. hello@getxyz.com or team@xyzagency.com

## Nordics and Baltics Angle

  These markets are underserved by quality email agencies.
  Most brands there are working with generalist agencies or
  doing email in-house badly.

  Adjust outbound angle for this market:

  Lead with: local market understanding + English fluency
  Reference: brands in their market you've worked with
             (or adjacent markets if none yet)
  Tone: slightly more formal than US/UK outreach
  Channel: email first, LinkedIn second — works well in Nordics

  Consider a separate sequence for this market with
  localised references and a softer sell approach.
